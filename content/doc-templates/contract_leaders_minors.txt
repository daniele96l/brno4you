This agreement is complementary to the Erasmus+ project application, the grant agreement concluded for the project, the Erasmus+ Programme Guide and the applicable Erasmus+ Quality Standards. 
This agreement is concluded between Brno for you, z.s. as the applicant/coordinating organisation and the group leader identified below.
This agreement specifies the role, responsibilities and obligations of a group leader accompanying participants under the age of 18 during the above-mentioned Erasmus+ youth mobility project.
The purpose of this agreement is to ensure the safety, wellbeing, proper supervision and protection of participants during travel, programme activities, accommodation time, breaks, free time / city breaks and emergency situations.
The project is coordinated in accordance with the applicable rules of the Erasmus+ Programme, the project documentation, the instructions of Brno for you, z.s. and the internal rules of the project.

Declaration of the group leader
I, the undersigned group leader, declare that I accept the responsibilities and obligations connected with the role of a group leader accompanying and supervising participants during the above-mentioned Erasmus+ youth mobility project.
I understand that, as a group leader of minor participants, I have an increased duty of care towards the minors in my group. I am expected to act responsibly, prevent foreseeable risks, supervise the minors appropriately and immediately report any concern to the project coordinator.
Main responsibilities and obligations
I understand and will follow the rules and guidelines of the Erasmus+ Programme, the project rules and the instructions of Brno for you, z.s.
I will actively participate in creating an open, inclusive, respectful and safe space for all participants.
I will participate in preparatory online meetings and follow the instructions of the applicant/coordinating organisation before, during and after the project.
I am aware of the aims of the project and will actively support participants in achieving them, including their learning process, self-development and Youthpass reflection.
I will attend the programme sessions and support participants to be fully involved in the activities. (help them to communicate with the other participants in a foreign language, explain them the situation in connection with their self-development and competences acquiring).
I will help participants understand the principles of non-formal education, key competences and the Youthpass process.
I will check the necessary documentation of the participants in my group, including parental/legal representative consent, insurance information, health information and travel documents.
I will support participants during travel and will immediately inform the project coordinator about travel delays, missing documents, health issues or any other relevant concern.
I will follow the instructions of the travel and check necessary documentation of the group (e.g. insurance card, consent of the legal representative…), assist participants in traveling and inform them about the reimbursement procedure.
I will keep under close watch the involved participants and encourage them to be polite and respectful towards each other. In case of any intentional damage on the property caused by participants, I will be responsible for delivering financial compensation from the participants.
I will ensure the safety of participants, respect the ideas and opinions of others, cultural differences, or different levels of language ability of other participants.
I am aware of special needs, health information, allergies, medication information, dietary needs and other important information shared for the participants in my group, and I will treat this information confidentially.
I will monitor the wellbeing, behaviour and group dynamics of the participants in my group during programme time, accommodation time, breaks, travel and free time.
I will carry out regular daily check-ins/evaluations with my national group and bring relevant observations, suggestions, concerns and needs to the leaders' meeting or directly to the project coordinator.
I will keep the project coordinator informed about any incident, risk, breach of rules, health concern, emotional distress, conflict or situation that may affect the safety, wellbeing or smooth implementation of the project.
I will support participants in planned follow-up and dissemination activities and provide Brno for you, z.s. with relevant links or information about completed dissemination activities.
I will provide the Applicant Organization with links where partner organisations, or the participants themselves, have disseminated the project.
I understand that during any free time / city break I remain responsible for the minors in my group and must ensure that they follow the agreed rules, stay within the agreed area and return at the agreed time.
I understand that I may not allow minor participants from my group to go alone during free time / city break, and I must ensure that they share their live location with me via WhatsApp for the whole free-time / city-break period.
I understand that the project applies zero tolerance towards drugs or illegal substances, alcohol consumption by minors, smoking/vaping by minors or in front of minors, violence, threats, bullying, harassment, discrimination, sexual misconduct or inappropriate sexualised behaviour, leaving the venue or agreed area without permission, damage to property, and any breach of the law, accommodation rules, programme rules or project rules; I will immediately report any breach or suspected breach to the project coordinator 
I will maintain professional boundaries with all participants, especially minors, and will not engage in or tolerate any romantic, sexual, manipulative, humiliating, discriminatory, violent, bullying, harassing or otherwise inappropriate behaviour; I will respect the privacy, dignity and personal data of minors, avoid being alone with a minor in a private or isolated space unless necessary for their safety or wellbeing, inform another leader or the project coordinator if such a situation occurs, and will not publish any photos, videos, names, health information, location data or other personal information about minors unless permitted by their legal representatives’ consent and the coordinator
I declare that I have not been convicted of any criminal offence that would make me unsuitable to work with children or young people, especially offences related to violence, abuse, sexual misconduct, drugs or endangering minors;
In case of injury, illness, mental distress, panic attack, suspected self-harm, intoxication, missing participant, violence, sexual misconduct or any other crisis situation, I will ensure immediate safety, inform the project coordinator without delay, call emergency services if the situation is serious.

I understand that serious breach of this agreement may result in my removal from the leader role, restriction of my participation in the project, notification of my sending organisation and other steps necessary to protect minor participants and the project.

Travel reimbursement and group obligations
I understand that, as a group leader, I am responsible for checking, collecting and submitting complete and correct travel reimbursement documentation for myself and for all participants in my national group. I also agree to remind, support and supervise the participants in fulfilling all administrative and project-related obligations, especially submitting complete travel documents, completing the follow-up plan approved during the mobility and carrying out the agreed dissemination activities. I understand that I am responsible for completing the final EU Survey sent to me by e-mail. I understand that reimbursement of travel costs for myself and/or my national group may be postponed, reduced or refused, fully or partially, if these documents or obligations are not properly fulfilled.

This agreement is concluded between the Group Leader and Brno for you, z.s. Both parties confirm that they understand their roles, responsibilities and communication duties during the project.

By signing this agreement, the group leader confirms that they have read, understood and agree to follow this agreement and Annex 1 - Leader Manual &amp; Checklist for Group Leaders.
