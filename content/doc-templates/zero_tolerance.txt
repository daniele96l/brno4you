This protocol is complimentary to the annexes of the KA1 project application of the Erasmus+ Programme of the EU and the respective funding contract signed between the Brno for you and the National Agency. It specifies the role and the duties of participants in a Youth Mobility Project in the Frame of ERASMUS+ Youth programme organized by Brno for you. The project is coordinated in accordance with the rules set out in the Erasmus+ Programme Guide and its Quality Standards. 

I, {{full_name}}                Date of birth: {{birth_date}}

hereby declare to follow and respect the below-mentioned rules. 
• No drugs.
• No smoking, vaping or use of nicotine products.
• No alcohol.
• No offensive, violent, aggressive or inappropriate behaviour.
• No verbal abuse, bullying, harassment, intimidation or discrimination of any kind.
• No inappropriate physical contact or sexual activity.
• No possession or use of weapons, dangerous objects or illegal substances.
• No leaving the venue, accommodation or group without permission.
• No inviting strangers or unauthorised persons to the venue or accommodation.
• Participants must follow the instructions of national leaders, project coordinator and staff.
• Participants must respect the daily programme, meeting times, curfew, accommodation rules and all safety instructions.
• No breaking of the law of the host country, the rules of the programme/project/accommodation or the rules set and agreed together during the project.
• Destruction of the property of others may result in the participant/parents/legal guardians being required to pay financial compensation.
• In case of any mental or physical discomfort, health problem, injury, fear, conflict or unsafe situation, the participant must inform the national leader or project coordinator immediately.
Any serious breach of these rules may result in exclusion from project activities, informing parents/legal guardians and, if necessary, arranging the participant’s return home at the parents’/legal guardians’ expense.
