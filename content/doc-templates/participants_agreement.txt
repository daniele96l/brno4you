This agreement is complementary to the Erasmus+ project application, the grant agreement concluded for the project, the Erasmus+ Programme Guide and the applicable Erasmus+ Quality Standards. It specifies the role, rights and duties of participants in the above-mentioned Youth Mobility Project organised by Brno for you, z.s. as the hosting organisation.

I, (participants name and surname)				Date of birth: 


hereby declare that I have read, understood and agree with this Participant Agreement. I declare that the information provided by me is true and complete and that I will follow the rules and instructions connected with my participation in the project.
1. General responsibilities of the participant
I declare and agree that I will:
follow the rules and guidelines of the Erasmus+ Programme, the project, the venue, the accommodation and the principles of good manners;
contribute to an open, respectful and safe environment by respecting other participants, cultures, opinions, identities, needs, boundaries and different levels of language ability;
be responsible for my own learning process and take part in reflection, evaluation and Youthpass-related activities if applicable;
follow the instructions of the hosting organisation, project coordinator, facilitators, national leader;
immediately inform the national leader or the project coordinator about any problem, concern, illness, injury, mental or physical discomfort, anxiety, distress or any other circumstance affecting my safety or participation;
have valid health/travel insurance for the hosting country for the whole duration of the project, including travel days, unless the hosting organisation has explicitly confirmed that such insurance is provided by them. If the participant is not able to arrange such insurance, they must inform the sending or the hosting organisation so that appropriate support or guidance can be provided;
take part in required follow-up and dissemination activities, including feedback, evaluation, sharing project outcomes and providing requested links, photos or other agreed outputs;
respect the night quiet hours from 23:00 to 7:00 and any additional quiet time rules set by the accommodation, hosting organisation or project team;
be responsible for my own personal belongings, valuables, travel documents, money, phone and other personal items.
Respect reasonable adaptation of the programme provided in the infopack, especially due to safety reasons, organisational needs, weather conditions, the needs of the group, group dynamics or other circumstances relevant to the quality and safe implementation of the project.
2. Zero tolerance rules
I understand and agree that the project has zero tolerance towards the following behaviour:
drugs or use of illegal substances;
alcohol consumption during workshops; if I am under 18, I am not allowed to drink alcohol at all;
smoking in prohibited areas or smoking by participants under 18;
violence, bullying, harassment, discrimination, hate speech, offensive behaviour or verbal abuse;
sexual misconduct, pressure, inappropriate touching or any behaviour violating the boundaries or dignity of another person;
theft, damage to property or misuse of property belonging to others;
breaking the law of the host country, the rules of the Erasmus+ Programme, the rules of the project, the rules of the accommodation or the rules agreed together during the project.
I understand that a serious breach of these rules may result in my exclusion from the project and may have financial consequences described in this agreement.
3. Attendance and absence from the programme
I agree to attend the whole programme and participate actively in all planned activities, unless I am excused in advance by the hosting organisation, national leader or project coordinator, or unless there is an accepted case of force majeure.
If I miss more than half of one programme day without prior approval or accepted force majeure, I understand that I may lose the right to reimbursement of my travel costs, either fully or partially. I also understand that the hosting organisation may claim the actual project costs incurred on my behalf, including but not limited to accommodation, meals, local transport and programme-related costs.
4. Cancellation, withdrawal and replacement
If I cancel my participation or withdraw from the project without a justified reason and without finding a suitable replacement approved by the hosting organisation, I understand and agree that the hosting organisation may claim from me the actual costs already incurred on my behalf, to the extent that such costs cannot be cancelled, refunded, recovered from another source or covered by the project.
5. Force majeure
Force majeure means an exceptional, unforeseeable situation or event beyond my control which prevents me from participating in the project or continuing my participation and which is not caused by my mistake, negligence or lack of preparation. Force majeure may include, for example, serious illness or injury, hospitalisation, death or serious illness of a close family member, official travel restrictions, cancellation of transport by the carrier, natural disaster, accident or other comparable serious circumstances. If I wish to rely on force majeure, I must inform the hosting organisation and my national leader immediately and provide relevant supporting documents without undue delay, such as a medical certificate, official confirmation from the carrier, police report, official authority statement or another relevant document. The hosting organisation will assess each case individually. 
6. Travel reimbursement
I understand that travel costs will be reimbursed only if I properly fulfil all administrative and project-related obligations connected with my participation in the project. These obligations include, in particular, submitting complete travel documentation, such as boarding passes, tickets, invoices, receipts, proof of payment, travel itinerary and any other documents requested by the hosting organisation; completing the follow-up plan approved on the final day of the mobility; implementing the agreed dissemination activities; and completing the final participant questionnaire (EU Survey) sent to me by e-mail.
If I fail to submit the required documents, complete the agreed follow-up plan, carry out the agreed dissemination activities, complete the EU Survey or otherwise fulfil the administrative and project-related obligations, I understand that my travel reimbursement may be reduced, postponed or refused.
Travel reimbursement will be made according to Erasmus+ rules, the approved project budget and the travel costs approved in advance by the hosting organisation. Reimbursement may be reduced or refused if the travel route, dates, means of transport or costs were not approved in advance, are not eligible under Erasmus+ rules or are not properly documented.
I understand and agree that all travel arrangements, including the travel route, dates, means of transport, costs and any additional green travel days, are subject to prior approval by the project coordinator or the hosting organisation. I must choose an economical, reasonable and purpose-oriented travel option directly connected with my participation in the project. The hosting organisation may reduce or refuse reimbursement of travel costs that are excessive, unnecessary, unreasonable, not approved in advance, not properly documented or not eligible under Erasmus+ rules.
I understand that taxi costs, fines, penalties, sanctions, excessive or overweight luggage fees, additional baggage not approved in advance, rebooking fees caused by my mistake and other costs caused by my negligence or personal choices are not eligible for reimbursement, unless explicitly approved in advance by the project coordinator or the hosting organisation due to objective and justified reasons.
I understand that additional green travel days may be claimed only if they are objectively necessary due to the availability, timing and reasonable organisation of suitable transport connections and only if they have been approved in advance by the project coordinator or the hosting organisation. Extra days chosen for personal convenience, tourism, holiday, recreation or other private reasons are not eligible for reimbursement or support.
I understand that the hosting organisation does not reimburse travel from or to a holiday, private trip, recreation stay or any other destination unrelated to the project, unless such route has been explicitly approved in advance by the project coordinator or the hosting organisation and is eligible under Erasmus+ rules. Travel costs are reimbursed only for travel directly and reasonably connected with participation in the project.
The hosting organisation reserves a reasonable period of time to check whether all conditions have been fulfilled and to process the reimbursement. I understand that travel reimbursement is not automatically due immediately after I have fulfilled the conditions. It will be paid after the project activity and after all required documents and obligations have been submitted, checked and approved.
7. Costs caused by breach of rules
I understand that if I seriously breach this agreement, the rules of the project, the rules of the accommodation, the instructions of the hosting organisation or national leader, or the law of the host country, I may be asked to leave the project.
In such a case, I understand that I may lose the right to reimbursement of travel costs and may be required to cover all costs caused by my behaviour, including but not limited to accommodation, meals, local transport, return travel, damage to property, cancellation fees and other costs incurred by the hosting organisation or third parties.
Damage to property may result in my obligation to financially compensate the damage in full.
8. Health, safety and emergency situations
I declare that I have provided true and complete information about my health condition, allergies, dietary restrictions, medication, mental or physical limitations and any other circumstances that may affect my safe participation in the project.
In case of emergency, I agree that the hosting organisation, national leader or project coordinator may take necessary steps to protect my health and safety, including contacting emergency services, arranging medical assistance and informing my emergency contact person.
I understand and agree that, if necessary for organisational, safety, medical or logistical reasons, I may be transported by a private car driven by an authorised person.
9. Audio-visual materials and personal data
I understand that photographs, videos and other audio-visual recordings may be taken during the project for documentation, reporting, educational, dissemination and promotional purposes related to the project, the Erasmus+ Programme, the hosting organisation, partner organisations and relevant public institutions.
I agree that audio-visual materials in which I appear may be used in printed and digital project materials, websites, social media, reports, presentations and other communication channels connected with the project and Erasmus+ dissemination activities.
The materials must be used in accordance with ethics, dignity and good manners and must not be used in a misleading, offensive or inappropriate context.
I understand that my personal data will be processed only for purposes connected with the organisation, implementation, reporting, safety, dissemination and administration of the project, in accordance with the applicable data protection rules and the information provided by the hosting organisation.
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Czech Republic, and any unresolved disputes shall be settled by the competent courts of the Czech Republic. 
By signing this agreement, I confirm that I have had the opportunity to ask questions and that I understand the possible consequences of breaching this agreement.
